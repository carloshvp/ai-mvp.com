AIMVP Mail Privacy Personal use

Privacy and Google data use

Effective 17 October 2026. This notice describes AIMVP Mail, a private email integration operated by Carlos Hernandez.

Access and purpose

The application accesses basic Google account identity and messages in the connected Gmail account, and can send email from that account. Message content and relevant context are used to carry out the account owner's requests, prepare responses, and send authorized mail.

Processing and service providers

Google hosts the mailbox and handles account authorization. Relevant message content, task context and generated responses may be processed by OpenAI to provide the requested functionality. Google and OpenAI handle data under their applicable terms and account controls. This notice does not claim that providers retain no data.

The operator does not sell mailbox data, use it for advertising, or intentionally use it to train a general-purpose AI model.

Storage and retention

Original messages and sent mail remain in Gmail under the account owner's retention settings. Relevant message-processing records, conversation history and generated files are held in private application storage until the operator removes them. No fixed automatic deletion period is configured.

Account controls and contact

The account owner can revoke access in Google Account connections, delete Gmail messages, and contact the operator to request removal of retained application records and generated files. Revoking access stops future mailbox access; it does not itself erase previously retained records.

For privacy or access questions, use the operator's existing contact channels.
