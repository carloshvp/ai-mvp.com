AIMVP Mail

A private email integration operated by Carlos Hernandez for his own account. It reads messages, helps with account-owner requests, and sends authorized email. It is not a public email service and has no public registration.

Google connection

The account owner authorizes access to basic Google account identity and Gmail read and send permissions. The connection does not request access to Drive, Calendar, mailbox deletion, account settings changes, or domain-wide delegation.
